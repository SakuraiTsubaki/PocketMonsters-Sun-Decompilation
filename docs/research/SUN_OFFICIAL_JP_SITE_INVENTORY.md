# Pokémon Sun — Japanese Official Site Inventory

## Purpose

This file inventories the Japanese first-party Pokémon Sun/Moon website as a **corpus**, not as a handful of representative citations. The Japanese site is the baseline official-public source tree for this project.

Source root:

- https://www.pokemon.co.jp/ex/sun_moon/

Inventory status values:

- `Discovered` — linked/indexed page confirmed to exist
- `Reviewed` — page body reviewed and relevant facts extracted
- `Cross-checked` — compared against at least one other first-party/technical source where appropriate
- `Applied` — findings propagated to the relevant version/data/system/reconstruction document

The counts below describe the currently reachable Japanese official site indexes. They do **not** mean the individual pages have all been fully reviewed yet.

## Overseas links exposed by the Japanese site

The Japanese site explicitly links to overseas Sun/Moon official sites. The currently recovered Chinese official sites include:

- Traditional Chinese: https://www.pokemon-sunmoon-cn.com/tc/
- Simplified Chinese: https://www.pokemon-sunmoon-cn.com/sc/

The Chinese site includes its own news tree, software/product pages, feature pages and region/hardware compatibility explanations. It must be surveyed independently rather than treated as a translation mirror.

The English official-site target must likewise be recovered and archived even where the current Japanese redirect no longer resolves cleanly.
